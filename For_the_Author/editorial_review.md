## Review assessment

The manuscript’s contribution is a trial-level comparison of intervention packages, delivery formats, and measured outcomes. It places community and health-service prevention evidence alongside the literature on clinical intervention. The revision adds trials from Côte d’Ivoire and Ethiopia, recognizes the Bandebereho follow-up as part of the original cohort, and replaces the draft’s vote count of favorable trials with study-specific comparisons. The source audit documents the changes and the exact outcome estimates used in the figure.

The two Ethiopian trials demonstrate why the outcome and setting matter. The Hadiya program concerns violence during pregnancy. Unite for a Better Life tested broader community education and reported different primary outcomes. The South African Couples Health CoOp study provides a direct joint-versus-separate contrast for a specified outcome. The comparison across significance levels in Unite for a Better Life was removed as evidence of a direct difference. The manuscript distinguishes estimated package effects from proposed mechanisms.
