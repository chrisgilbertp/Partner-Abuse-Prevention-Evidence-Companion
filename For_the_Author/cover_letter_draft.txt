Please consider “Couple-Based Programs for Preventing Partner Abuse in Sub-Saharan Africa” as a literature review for Partner Abuse.

The review examines nine randomized trials across seven countries. It distinguishes the effect of offering a prevention program from the narrower effect of delivering it jointly to both partners. It also considers what the trials can establish about safety and applicability to counseling. The paper connects prevention evidence from community and health-service settings with questions relevant to researchers, intervention providers, clinicians, and advocates.
